Tutor Marketplace Platform
Product Requirements Document - Lead Purchase & Smart Matching System
Version 1.0  |  Prepared for development, design, and stakeholder review
1. Project Overview
Purpose: The platform connects parents seeking tuition with qualified tutors through an automated, lead-based matching system. Instead of manually browsing tutor listings, parents post a requirement once; the system identifies and surfaces the requirement to relevant tutors, who then purchase access and apply.
Business Model: Revenue is generated through a coin-based lead purchase system. Tutors buy coins in packages and spend coins to unlock parent leads that match their teaching profile. Parents use the platform free of charge.
User Roles:
● Parent - posts tuition requirements, reviews tutor applications, books and rates tutors.
● Tutor - creates a verified profile, purchases matched leads, applies, delivers classes, and is rated.
● Admin (Super Admin) - has full platform access, configuration, and override authority.
● Sub Admin - has role-restricted access to specific operational modules.
Platform Goals:
● Reduce parent effort in finding a suitable tutor by pre-filtering candidates through automated matching.
● Give tutors access only to leads relevant to their subject, location, and pricing.
● Maintain lead quality by capping the number of competing tutors per lead.
● Build trust through verification, ratings, and a transparent booking and feedback cycle.
2. Parent Workflow
2.1 Registration
Parents can register using any of the following methods:
● Mobile number with OTP verification (primary method).
● Email and password.
● Google Sign-In (OAuth).
Assumption: mobile OTP is treated as the primary identity for account recovery and communication, since WhatsApp/SMS is the main outreach channel for Indian parents. This is not stated explicitly in the source material and should be confirmed.
2.2 Parent Profile
● Profile fields: name, contact number, email, city/area, preferred language.
● A parent account can hold multiple student profiles (for multiple children), each with its own class, board, and subject needs.
● Address is captured at the student-profile level to support accurate distance-based matching.
2.3 Posting a Requirement
The requirement form captures the following fields:
● Subject(s) - one or more, selected from a fixed taxonomy.
● Class / Grade - including competitive exam tracks (JEE, NEET, CA, coding, music, language, etc.).
● Board - CBSE, ICSE, State Board, IB, or Other.
● Mode - Online, Offline, or Either.
● Location - required for Offline/Either mode; used for radius-based matching.
● Budget - parent's expected fee range.
● Preferred timings - days and time slots.
● Tutor preference - Male, Female, or No preference.
● Language preference.
● Additional notes - free text.
Validation: Subject, Class, Mode, and Budget are mandatory. Location is mandatory unless Mode is Online only. Budget must be a positive number within platform-defined minimum/maximum bounds per class level.
Requirement statuses: Draft → Active → Matching → Applications Received → Booked → Completed → Closed/Expired.
On Submit: The system validates the form, creates a new lead record with status Active, timestamps it, and immediately triggers the Tutor Matching System (Section 3). The parent is shown a confirmation screen with expected response time.
3. Tutor Matching System
When a lead is created, the system evaluates all eligible tutors against the following parameters:
● Distance from student location vs. tutor's declared teaching radius.
● Subject and class-level expertise match.
● Budget compatibility (tutor's fee range vs. parent's budget).
● Tutor ratings and review count.
● Profile completion percentage.
● Verification/KYC status.
● Availability against the parent's preferred timing.
● Mode match (Online/Offline).
Ranking priority (highest to lowest weight):
● 1. Verified tutors are ranked above unverified tutors.
● 2. Closer tutors (within initial radius) are ranked above tutors reached via radius expansion.
● 3. Higher-rated tutors are ranked above lower-rated tutors, subject to a minimum review count to avoid new-tutor bias.
● 4. Higher profile completion improves ranking among otherwise-equal tutors.
Only tutors that pass all mandatory filters (subject, class, mode, budget floor) are notified; ranking then determines the order in which the lead is surfaced to them.
4. Tutor Module
4.1 Registration & KYC
● Registration via mobile OTP, email, or Google Sign-In.
● KYC: government ID upload, address proof, and a live selfie for verification.
● A tutor cannot purchase leads until KYC is submitted; a Verification Badge is granted only after admin approval.
4.2 Profile
● Qualifications and certificates (uploaded documents).
● Years of experience.
● Subjects taught and class levels covered.
● Teaching radius (in km) for offline classes.
● Availability calendar.
● Languages spoken.
● Introduction video (optional but boosts profile completion score and ranking).
4.3 Tutor Dashboard
● Wallet - current coin balance and quick top-up.
● Purchased Leads - list of unlocked leads with parent contact and application status.
● Applications - status of each submitted application (Pending, Shortlisted, Rejected, Hired).
● Analytics - leads purchased, conversion rate, average rating, profile views.
● Notifications - new matched leads, application updates, booking confirmations, payment/wallet alerts.
5. Wallet & Coin System
● Wallet displays current balance and full transaction history (purchases, deductions, refunds, bonuses).
● Coins are sold in fixed packages via an integrated payment gateway (UPI/cards/net banking).
● Bonus coins may be credited on first purchase or large top-ups; referral coins are credited when a referred tutor completes registration and KYC.
Refund policy (recommended default): Coins are refunded to wallet only - not as cash - and only for lead-level issues (see Section 6.9). Coin package purchases themselves are non-refundable once coins are credited, consistent with standard digital-goods practice; this should be confirmed against applicable consumer protection requirements before launch.
5.1 Dynamic Lead Pricing
Coin cost per lead is fixed per class/category tier, as confirmed. Pricing increases with academic level and specialization, since higher classes and competitive exams require more experienced, harder-to-source tutors, and yield higher parent budgets:
CategoryIndicative Coin CostRationaleClass 1-5LowHigh tutor supply, lower parent budgets, easier to fill.Class 6-8Low-MediumSlightly higher subject specificity.Class 9-10MediumBoard exam stakes increase demand for experienced tutors.Class 11-12Medium-HighSubject specialization and board-exam pressure.JEE / NEETPremiumHighly specialized tutors, high parent budgets, high competition among tutors.CA / ProfessionalPremiumNiche expertise, limited tutor pool.CodingMedium-HighSkill-based, moderate specialist supply.Music / ArtsMediumSkill-based, moderate specialist supply.LanguageLow-MediumBroad tutor availability.
Exact coin values per tier are a pricing/business decision to be finalized separately (e.g., via a pricing configuration table in the Admin Panel) rather than hard-coded, so Admin can adjust tiers without a release.
6. Lead Purchase System
● Visibility: only tutors who pass the mandatory matching filters (Section 3) and hold sufficient wallet balance can see and purchase a given lead.
● Tutors who do not meet subject/class/mode/budget criteria never see the lead.
● Maximum 5 tutors may purchase a single lead. Once 5 purchases occur, the lead is closed to further purchases.
Lead expiry (recommended default): 48 hours from creation, or earlier if 5 tutors have purchased it. This is short enough to keep leads fresh for the parent and long enough to allow radius expansion to complete; the exact window should be tuned post-launch using purchase-time analytics.
● Purchase deducts coins from the tutor's wallet immediately and atomically (no purchase is confirmed without a successful deduction).
● On purchase: parent's contact details are unlocked for that tutor, and the tutor is enabled to submit an application.
● Lead statuses: Active → Partially Purchased (1-4 tutors) → Fully Purchased (5 tutors) → Expired/Archived.
● Radius expansion: if fewer than 5 tutors purchase within the initial radius before a defined checkpoint (e.g., 25% of the expiry window elapsed), the radius auto-expands in fixed increments (5 km → 10 km → 15 km) until 5 purchases occur or the maximum radius is reached.
● Duplicate prevention: a tutor cannot purchase the same lead twice; the system checks tutor ID against the lead's purchase list before allowing a transaction.
Invalid lead refund policy (recommended default): A tutor may request a refund within 24 hours of purchase if the parent's number is unreachable/invalid, or if the requirement is confirmed duplicate/spam by Admin. Refunds are credited as coins to the tutor's wallet after review. Refunds are not granted simply because a parent does not respond or does not select that tutor, since contact was successfully made.
7. Parent Receives Applications
As tutors purchase the lead and apply, their profiles appear on the parent's dashboard in ranked order (see Section 3 ranking logic).
Comparison parameters shown per tutor:
● Rating and number of reviews
● Experience (years)
● Fees
● Distance from student
● Qualification
● Languages spoken
● Verification status
● Profile completion percentage
● Trial class availability
Parent actions per tutor: Shortlist, Reject, Hire, Schedule Trial, Chat, Call.
8. Booking Workflow
● Online classes: once hired, the tutor shares a Google Meet/Zoom link through the platform; the link and schedule are visible to both parties.
● Offline classes: both parties receive confirmed date, time, and location details.
● Trial booking: parent can request a trial session before full booking; trial completion is tracked separately from regular class completion.
● Rescheduling: either party can propose a new time; the other party must accept for the change to be confirmed.
● Cancellation: allowed up to a defined cutoff (e.g., 2 hours before class); late cancellations are logged against the cancelling party's reliability score.
● Notifications are sent to both parties on booking confirmation, reschedule, cancellation, and upcoming-class reminders.
Booking statuses: Requested → Confirmed → Rescheduled (optional) → Completed → Cancelled.
9. Feedback & Rating System
Only verified, completed bookings unlock the review prompt, preventing fake or pre-emptive reviews.
● Parent rates tutor on: Teaching Quality, Communication, Punctuality, Overall Experience.
● Tutor rates parent on: Communication, Student Behaviour, Overall Experience.
Impact of ratings:
● Directly affects tutor visibility and ranking in future matching (Section 3).
● Affects lead priority - higher-rated tutors are notified earlier in the purchase window.
● Feeds into Verification Badge and loyalty tier eligibility (Section 10).
Fake review prevention: reviews are tied to a booking ID that must have status Completed; one review per booking; reviews cannot be edited after 48 hours to prevent manipulation after disputes.
10. Rewards & Loyalty
10.1 Parent Rewards
● Discount coupons for future bookings.
● Wallet-style credits usable toward platform service charges, where applicable.
● Promotional offers tied to festivals/seasons.
● Reduced service charges for repeat bookings.
10.2 Tutor Rewards
● Discounted lead prices for consistently high ratings.
● Bonus wallet credits for milestones (e.g., 10 completed bookings).
● Higher priority in lead matching and better search visibility.
● Exclusive promotional benefits (e.g., Featured Tutor placement).
● Referral Program: both referrer and referee receive bonus coins once the referee completes registration and KYC.

The Super Admin has full platform access and the following module-level capabilities:

12. Sub Admin
Sub Admin accounts are role-restricted and can only access the modules assigned to their role by the Super Admin. Confirmed roles:
RoleModule AccessSupportSupport Tickets, read-only access to Parent/Tutor profiles for issue resolution.VerificationTutor KYC review and approval, Verification Badge management.FinanceWallet Management, Payments, Refunds, financial Reports.OperationsLead Management, radius/expiry overrides, dispute handling on bookings.MarketingCoupons, Notifications (campaign-level), CMS promotional content.
Each Sub Admin's actions are logged against their account for audit purposes, and no Sub Admin role includes access to platform-wide Settings or the ability to create other admin accounts.
13. Super Admin - Full Platform Access
In addition to the capabilities listed in Section 11, the Super Admin role has unrestricted, non-delegable authority over the entire platform:
● Manage all Parent, Tutor, and Sub Admin accounts.
● Create and deactivate Sub Admin accounts and assign/revoke their permissions.
● Modify coin pricing tiers and package pricing at any time.
● Configure the tutor matching algorithm's parameters and ranking weights.
● Configure wallet rules: bonus structures, referral values, refund limits.
● Manage payment gateway integrations and credentials.
● View complete audit logs of all admin and Sub Admin actions.
● Manage platform-wide settings (lead expiry defaults, max tutors per lead, radius increments).
● Override any operation performed by any Sub Admin.
14. Business Rules
● A maximum of 5 tutors can purchase a single lead.
● Coins are deducted immediately and atomically upon lead purchase.
● Refunds are issued as coins, and only for invalid, duplicate, or unreachable-contact leads - not for parent non-response after a successful contact unlock.
● Search radius expands automatically in fixed increments (5 km → 10 km → 15 km) if the purchase target is not met within a defined checkpoint.
● Higher-rated tutors receive better visibility and earlier notification in the purchase window.
● Verified tutors rank above unverified tutors regardless of other ranking factors.
● Profile completion percentage is a tie-breaking ranking factor among otherwise comparable tutors.
● Admin (Super Admin and authorized Sub Admins per role) can manually refund, close, or reassign leads.
● Leads that reach their expiry window without full purchase are automatically archived.
● Coin package purchases are non-refundable once credited; lead-level refunds are credited back as coins only.
● Parents may edit only minor fields (preferred timing, additional notes) once a lead has active tutor purchases; core fields (subject, class, mode, location, budget) are locked to preserve match integrity for tutors who already purchased.
