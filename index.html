Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers for Parents & Tutors

Is ApnaTutorHub free for parents?
Yes, completely free. Post your tuition requirement, receive verified tutor profiles, and connect directly — no platform fees, ever. Parents pay only the tutor's agreed hourly rate.
How are tutors verified on ApnaTutorHub?
Every tutor submits identity proof (Aadhaar), educational qualifications, and teaching experience. Our team manually reviews each submission before issuing the Verified Tutor badge.
Can I choose between home tuition and online classes?
Absolutely. When posting your requirement, simply select your preferred mode — home tuition, live online classes, or both. Tutors who match your preference will reach out.
What subjects and school boards are supported?
We cover all major subjects for CBSE, ICSE, IGCSE, and all State Boards across India, plus competitive entrance exams like JEE Mains, JEE Advanced, NEET, and CUET.
How do I connect with a tutor after posting?
Verified tutors who match your requirement will reach out directly to you. You review their profiles, ratings, and parent reviews, then choose who to engage. No middlemen involved.
